Free QR Code Creator

Free QR Code Creator lets you design and download QR codes entirely in your browser. Choose what to encode, customize colors and dot style, then save a sharp image for menus, posters, packaging, or digital sharing. The same site also includes a barcode generator and a camera-based QR scanner when you need them.

For step-by-step help with common use cases, see our guides on how to create a Wi-Fi QR code and how to make a QR code for a website. More free browser tools are listed on web-free.site.

How to create a QR code

  1. Open the Create tab at the top of this page.
  2. Pick a data type (for example URL, Wi‑Fi, or plain text) and fill in the fields.
  3. Adjust foreground and background colors, dot shape, and optional logo if you want a branded look.
  4. Preview the code in the panel, then download it as PNG or SVG.
  5. Scan the finished code with your phone before printing or publishing to confirm it opens the right destination.

QR code types supported here

This generator encodes the following payload formats:

  • URL — website or landing-page links (http and https).
  • Plain text — any short message or label.
  • Wi‑Fi — network name, password, and WPA/WEP/nopass settings for guest access.
  • vCard — contact name, organization, phone, and email.
  • Email — mailto links with optional subject and body.
  • SMS — text messages with a preset recipient and message.
  • Phone — tap-to-call telephone numbers.
  • WhatsApp — chat links with an optional prefilled message.
  • Location — geographic coordinates for maps apps.
  • Event — calendar entries with title, time, and place.
  • Menu PDF — a URL pointing to a PDF menu or document.

Tips for readable, scannable codes

Use enough physical size on print: very small codes are harder for cameras to read. Keep strong contrast between dark modules and the background; light-on-light or low-contrast color pairs reduce scan success. Leave quiet zone (empty margin) around the code—do not crop tightly against the edge. Always test with a real phone at the distance viewers will use. Static codes you export from this tool do not expire by themselves; they work until the underlying URL, Wi‑Fi password, or text you encoded changes.

Frequently asked questions

Is Free QR Code Creator really free?

Yes. The QR generator, barcode tools, and scanner are free to use with no subscription and no payment required.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No. You can create and download QR codes immediately in your browser without registering or logging in.

Do the QR codes expire?

Static QR codes you download do not expire on their own. They keep working as long as the encoded link, text, or Wi‑Fi details remain valid.

Is my QR code data uploaded to your servers?

No. Encoding and image generation run entirely in your browser. We do not receive or store the content you put into a QR code.

Which file formats can I download?

You can download each QR code as a PNG image or as SVG vector graphics, ready for print or the web.

What types of QR codes does this app support?

This tool supports URL or website links, plain text, Wi‑Fi network login, vCard contacts, email, SMS, phone numbers, WhatsApp chats, map locations, calendar events, and links to a menu PDF.
